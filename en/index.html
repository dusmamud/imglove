Frequently asked questions

Are these image tools really free?

Yes. All tools on ImgLove are completely free with no hidden limits and no watermarks on your images.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using modern web technology. Your files never leave your device.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, AVIF and HEIC (iPhone photos) for input. You can export to JPG, PNG or WEBP.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no server-side limit since nothing is uploaded. Very large images may take longer to process on slower devices.
